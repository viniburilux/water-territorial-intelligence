# Water-domain lineage

HydraLux, WaterLux and LuxSense are related design lineage and future sensing directions. They are not primary empirical evidence for the current WTI fixtures.

The current WTI evidence chain is based on territorial representations, TTI/STT, Sentinel-derived context, historical visual investigation, temporal trajectories and provenance contracts. Sensor deployment, groundwater chemistry, field validation and operational users remain future validation targets unless independently documented.
