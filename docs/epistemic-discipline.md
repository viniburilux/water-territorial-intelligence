# Epistemic discipline

WTI explicitly separates what the evidence establishes from what it suggests and what remains unknown.

```text
OBSERVATION
    ↓
EVIDENCE
    ↓
CLAIM
    ↓
INFERENCE
    ↓
HYPOTHESIS
    ↓
GAP
    ↓
NEXT ACTION
```

A territorial transformation signal is not automatically a water-level measurement, water-quality measurement, causal explanation or legal finding. WTI preserves the chain and makes the missing evidence operational through a Research Move.

WTI also distinguishes four capability levels:

- **Demonstrated capability:** directly executed in an existing artifact.
- **Transferable capability:** demonstrated in another territorial or environmental context and architecturally reusable.
- **Compositional / architectural capability:** produced by connecting existing mechanisms into the WTI contract.
- **Future validation:** requiring new experiments, field data, partners or operational deployment.

The five fixtures have different historical depths. Executable means that a frozen observation can be loaded by the adapter and produce a machine-readable Investigation State and Research Move. It does not mean that all raw historical inputs are redistributed in this repository.
