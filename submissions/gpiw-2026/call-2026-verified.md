# GPIW 2026 call - verified parameters

Checked against live sources on 2026-09-28. The application form on the official platform is the final authority; this note records what the public call pages stated on that date.

- **Application window:** 8 June - 30 September 2026. Submissions close 30 September 2026 at 23:59 Arabian Standard Time (UTC+3). Late submissions are not considered.
- **Tracks:** TRL 1-3 Research and Proof of Concept; TRL 4-7 Development and Demonstration; Special Mention for TRL 8-9 / market-ready submissions.
- **Awards:** USD 100,000 Grand Prize (one for TRL 1-3, one for TRL 4-7); USD 50,000 Impact Medals (six per track); Special Mentions carry recognition and networking rather than formal prize money. Piloting support up to USD 250,000 is described for Grand Prize winners and selected medal winners.
- **Required submission:** a written proposal answering the application questions; a short pitch deck (PDF or PPT); a pitch video (mp4, about 2 minutes, under 50 MB). Optional: supporting documentation (PDF).
- **Evaluation framing:** fit within one of the prize categories and the correct TRL track; innovation, technical merit and potential impact on water challenges; a credible path toward piloting, scaling or deployment appropriate to the readiness level.
- **Multiple submissions:** at most three per solver; only the final three submitted are considered.
- **Process:** submission and jury evaluation are remote; the award ceremony later in 2026 requires attendance or a designated representative.
